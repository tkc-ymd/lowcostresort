Locations

We currently operate two resort hotels in Okinawa.

LOW COST RESORT MANZA BEACH

Steps away from the beautiful beach.

Your smart resort base to explore Onna Village.
A boutique hotel located in the heart of Onna Village, Okinawa's premier west coast resort area. It boasts a prime location just a few minutes' walk from Nabee Beach, a beautiful stretch of white sand and emerald green sea. All rooms feature comfortable tatami floors for a relaxing barefoot experience.

LOW COST RESORT NAHA AIRPORT

Convenient access from Naha Airport.

An urban resort to easily enjoy the city and beach.
A boutique hotel located near Naha Airport, making it the perfect base for sightseeing in Naha City, including Kokusai Street. Although in the city center, the beautiful white sand of Naminoue Beach is within walking distance. All rooms feature comfortable tatami floors for a relaxing barefoot experience.
