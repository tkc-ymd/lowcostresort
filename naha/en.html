Low Cost Resort Naha Airport

An urban resort to easily enjoy both the city and the beach. Conveniently located near Naha Airport, it is the perfect base for sightseeing in Naha City, including Kokusai Street. Although in the heart of the city, the beautiful white sand of Naminoue Beach is within walking distance. All rooms feature comfortable tatami floors for a relaxing barefoot experience.

Rooms

Japanese-Style Room (Washitsu)

A relaxing room designed for comfort and simplicity. Sleep on thick futons on tatami mats, allowing you to stretch your legs and unwind in a spacious atmosphere.
Enjoy sightseeing and activities during the day, and relax on the tatami mats at night to soothe your travel fatigue—the perfect base for your free island journey. Ideal and comfortable for families with small children or groups of friends.
Capacity: 2 Adults (Children sharing bed stay for free)

Premium Free Services

Free Lounge

Complimentary warm coffee, tea, and cold soft drinks are available in our guest-only lounge. Enjoy a relaxing moment in the lounge, or take drinks back to your room. Bringing in your own food is also welcome.

Shampoo Bar

Choose from a wide variety of popular shampoos and conditioners to suit your mood. A great chance to try out items you've been curious about. No need to return them—simply leave them in your room upon checkout.

Bicycle Rental

Complimentary rental bicycles are available for guest use. Highly popular for quick trips around Naha City. Please ask the front desk to use them (bicycles must be returned by 22:00).

Shared Facilities

Laundry Room

A guest-only free laundry room is available (8:00 - 21:00). Washing machines (with dryer functions) are free to use, and laundry detergent is provided. Travel light and keep your clothes fresh during your stay.

FAQ

Q.What are the front desk hours?
The front desk is open from 9:00 to 22:00.
If the staff is away from the desk, they are still on site; please call them using the bell on the counter.
Q.Is there internet/Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel and in all guest rooms.
We upgraded our connection in July 2024 to provide an even faster, more stable internet environment.
Q.Can I leave my luggage before check-in or after check-out?
Yes, we offer luggage storage. Please ask our front desk staff.
Q.Is there a smoking area?
The hotel is entirely non-smoking. A smoking area is located near the main entrance outside.
Q.Are there convenience stores or supermarkets nearby?
Yes, the following shops are within walking distance:
  • Convenience Store: FamilyMart Tsuji 1-Chome (Open 24 hours, stocks local Okinawa products and souvenirs).
  • Supermarket: Nishimachi Ryubo (Open 9:00 - 24:00, great for fresh ingredients and local foods).
Q.What amenities and room equipment are provided?
We provide:
  • Amenities: Shampoo, conditioner, body soap, bath amenities, bath towel, slippers.
  • Room Equipment: Hair dryer, electric kettle, refrigerator, flat-screen TV, air conditioner (heating/cooling), bathtub, shower, toilet, desk, linens.
Q.Is there room cleaning during my stay?
We do not clean rooms during your stay. Fresh towels and amenities are available for exchange at the front desk free of charge.
Q.Can children share beds?
Children sharing beds stay for free regardless of the number of children. Extra futons are not provided.
The number of futons provided matches the number of paying adults.
Q.Can I walk to Kokusai Street?
Yes, it is within walking distance (approx. 15-20 minutes).
Using our free rental bicycles makes it even faster and is highly recommended for exploring the area and local alleys.
Q.Can I walk to the beach?
Yes, the beautiful white sand of "Naminoue Beach" is about an 11-minute walk away.
It is a popular spot in Naha City to enjoy the emerald green sea and swimming.
Q.Are there any house rules?
Please check the evacuation route when you enter your room.
To ensure comfort for all guests, non-guests are not allowed in the lounge. Please note that rental items and drinks are subject to availability.
Q.What is the nearest monorail station, and how far is it?
The nearest station is "Asahibashi Station" on the Okinawa Monorail (Yui Rail).
It is about a 12-15 minute walk from the hotel.
