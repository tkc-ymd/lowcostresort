Low Cost Resort Manza Beach

A beach resort located in the heart of the Manza Resort Area in Onna Village, Okinawa. Just steps away from the emerald green Nabee Beach. Located right in the center of Okinawa's premier resort village, "Low Cost Resort Manza Beach" is the perfect base to explore the island and enjoy a relaxing resort stay at a reasonable price.

Rooms

Japanese-Style Room (Washitsu)

A relaxing room featuring the pleasant texture of tatami mats and a simple, warm Japanese atmosphere. Sleep comfortably on thick futons, making it a popular choice for families with small children or groups of friends. After playing at the beach or sightseeing, stretch your legs on the tatami mats and enjoy a relaxing "island time."
Capacity: 3 Adults (Children sharing bed stay for free)

Japanese-Western Room (Wayo-shitsu)

A comfortable room combining the cozy sleep of sofa beds with the relaxation of a tatami space. Enjoy sleeping on comfortable sofa beds while using the tatami area to drink tea, chat, or organize your luggage. A highly functional and comfortable room blending Japanese warmth with Western utility.
Capacity: 5 Adults (Children sharing bed stay for free)

Premium Free Services

To support your freedom and exploration in Okinawa, Low Cost Resort Manza Beach offers a range of complimentary premium services to enrich your stay.

Free Lounge

24-Hour Free Drink Lounge
Enjoy warm coffee, tea, and cold soft drinks anytime in our guest-only lounge. Feel free to take drinks back to your room, or bring in your own food to enjoy.

Beach Goods Rental

We offer free rentals of large floats and beach gear to use at Nabee Beach, just a 5-minute walk away. No need to pack bulky gear—travel light and enjoy the emerald green sea. Please ask our staff at the front desk.

Shampoo Bar

Choose from a wide variety of popular shampoos and conditioners to suit your mood. A great chance to try out items you've been curious about. No need to return them—simply leave them in your room upon checkout.

Bicycle Rental

Complimentary rental bicycles are available for exploring the area. Perfect for visiting local restaurants, convenience stores, or taking a scenic ride around the beautiful Manza area.
*Please return bicycles by 22:00.

Shared Facilities

Guest Parking

We offer a spacious, free on-site parking lot. Guests can park one vehicle per room for free, making it ideal for road trips and rental car tours around Okinawa. Please let the front desk know upon check-in.

Laundry Room

A guest-only free laundry room is available (8:00 - 21:00). Washing machines and dryers are free to use, and laundry detergent is provided. Wash and dry your swimsuits or towels easily, allowing you to travel with less luggage.

Foot Wash Station

An outdoor foot wash station is provided. After playing at Nabee Beach, rinse off sand from your feet, sandals, or floats here so you can return to your room clean and sand-free.

FAQ

Q.What are the front desk hours?
The front desk is open from 15:00 to 22:00.
For security, the main entrance is locked after 22:00. Please use the back entrance near the parking lot for night entry.
In case of emergency, please call the emergency contact number posted at the front desk.
Q.Is there parking available?
Yes, we offer free parking on site. Please inform the front desk during check-in.
One car per room is free. Additional cars will be charged 1,500 yen per night.
Q.Is there internet/Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel and in all guest rooms.
Q.Is there a smoking area?
The hotel is entirely non-smoking. A smoking area is located near the main entrance outside.
Q.Is there a laundry room?
Yes, we have a guest-only free laundry room. Dryers and laundry detergent are also provided.
Hours are 8:00 to 21:00.
Please rinse off sand from swimsuits before washing.
Q.What amenities and room equipment are provided?
We provide the following:
  • Amenities: Shampoo, conditioner, body soap, bath amenities, bath towel, face towel, slippers.
  • Room Equipment: Hair dryer, electric kettle, refrigerator, flat-screen TV, air conditioner (heating/cooling), bathtub, shower, toilet, desk, linens.
Q.Is there room cleaning during my stay?
We do not clean rooms during your stay. Fresh towels and amenities are available for exchange at the front desk free of charge.
Q.Can children share beds?
Children sharing beds stay for free regardless of the number of children. Extra futons are not provided.
If you need extra futons, they can be rented for 2,000 yen per set.
Q.Tell me about the drink lounge.
We offer complimentary warm coffee, tea, and cold soft drinks in the lounge 24 hours a day for our guests. You can also take drinks to your room.
Q.What rental items are available?
We offer free rental bicycles, beach floats/gear for Nabee Beach, and a custom shampoo bar.
Q.How do I get to the hotel from Naha Airport?
The "Okinawa Airport Shuttle" (highway bus) is highly convenient.
It takes about 108 minutes to reach "Nabee Beach Mae" or "Manza Beach Mae" bus stops, which are located right near the hotel.
Q.Are there any house rules?
Please check the evacuation route when you enter your room.
Please keep TV volumes low after 21:00 so other guests can rest. Please also stay quiet in the lounge after 21:00.
